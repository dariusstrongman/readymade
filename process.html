Booking process

From quote to delivered files in 48 hours.

No mystery process. Here is exactly what happens after you submit a quote request.

Get a QuoteCompare packages
1

Quote request

Submit the form on the quote page with property details, package preference, and access notes.

Takes 2 minutes.
2

Email reply within 24 hours

We confirm availability, walk through scope, and answer questions. If add-ons would help the listing we say so. If they would not we say that too.

Same business day usually.
3

Deposit + booking agreement

20% deposit secures the date. You receive a booking agreement by email covering scope, weather policy, file ownership, and cancellation terms. Replying with acceptance is the binding.

2 minutes after scope is locked.
4

Shoot day

We arrive at the scheduled window with camera, drone, lighting, and lenses. Supra eKEY access handled. No need for you to be on-site unless you want to be.

90 minutes on average.
5

Edit + delivery

Color graded photos and edited video delivered the morning after the shoot. NTREIS-ready files for MLS, social-cut variants for Reels and TikTok, drone aerials when applicable.

Next morning target.
6

Balance + files

Balance invoice arrives with the delivery link. Files release within minutes of payment. Download window is 30 days; save copies to your own storage before then.

Same day.
