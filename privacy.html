Privacy Policy

ReadyMadeVideo is a DBA/trade name of Stromation LLC. This page explains the practical rules that apply when visitors use the website or when services are requested after booking becomes available.

Effective date: June 5, 2026.

Scope: This Privacy Policy covers information collected through ReadyMadeVideo website forms, emails, social links, and future booking requests.

1. Business identity

ReadyMadeVideo is a DBA/trade name of Stromation LLC. References to "ReadyMadeVideo," "we," "our," or "us" mean Stromation LLC doing business as ReadyMadeVideo.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information that visitors or clients provide directly, such as name, email address, phone number, brokerage, property address, preferred package, listing notes, access instructions, add-ons, and message content. We may also receive basic technical information such as browser type, device information, referral pages, and general website analytics if analytics tools are enabled.

3. How we use information

UsePurpose
Responding to inquiriesTo answer questions, prepare scheduling, and follow up about services.
Project planningTo evaluate property scope, access needs, add-ons, shoot timing, and delivery requirements.

4. How information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the business, such as email, form, hosting, automation, storage, payment, editing, analytics, or delivery tools. We may also share information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or with a client's permission.

5. Payment processing

We use Stripe for payment processing. Stripe handles card details under its own privacy policy, and ReadyMadeVideo does not store payment card information. Stripe states that it maintains PCI-DSS compliance for payment-card data handled through its platform.

6. Property media and listing information

Real estate media can include the property address, exterior views, interior views, signage, vehicles, people, pets, or neighboring areas if they are visible during the shoot. Clients should remove sensitive items and notify occupants or affected parties before the appointment.

7. Retention

We keep inquiry records, project notes, and delivered media for as long as reasonably needed for business operations, client support, portfolio documentation, legal compliance, and recordkeeping. Raw and working files may be deleted according to storage needs unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

8. Choices and privacy requests

You may contact contact@readymadevideo.com to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information where applicable. We may need to verify the request before acting on it, and some records may be retained when required for legal, security, or business reasons.

For California residents, the California Consumer Privacy Act may provide rights to know, delete, correct, and limit the use of certain personal information. Texas residents may have similar rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. Contact contact@readymadevideo.com to exercise available privacy rights.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No website, email system, storage platform, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Children

The website and services are intended for real estate professionals and property-marketing clients. They are not directed to children.

11. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as tools, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above shows when this version was last updated.
