Refund Policy

ReadyMadeVideo is a DBA/trade name of Stromation LLC. This page explains the practical rules that apply when visitors use the website or when services are requested after booking becomes available.

Effective date: June 5, 2026.

Booking status: Online booking is currently Coming Soon. This policy is prepared for service requests once booking becomes available or when projects are accepted separately in writing.

1. Business identity

ReadyMadeVideo is a DBA/trade name of Stromation LLC. References to "ReadyMadeVideo," "we," "our," or "us" mean Stromation LLC doing business as ReadyMadeVideo.

2. General refund approach

Accepted packages require a 20% deposit to reserve the shoot date and production slot unless separate written terms are approved. Because listing-media work involves reserved shoot time, travel, production planning, editing, and digital delivery, refunds depend on the project stage, cancellation timing, access conditions, and whether work has already begun or been delivered.

3. Cancellations and rescheduling

SituationPolicy
Booking not yet acceptedNo project is confirmed and no service fee is due unless separately agreed in writing.
Client cancels before production beginsAny deposit refund or credit will depend on notice timing, reserved calendar time, travel, and preparation already completed.
Weather, access, or safety issueWe may reschedule, adjust the scope, or provide a credit when weather, access, airspace, or safety conditions prevent completion.
Work already captured or editedRefunds are generally not available for completed capture, editing time, delivered files, or project work already performed.

4. Delivery concerns

Delivery times are targets and can be affected by access, scope, weather, airspace restrictions, client responsiveness, file-transfer issues, and technical conditions. If a delivery issue occurs, the client should contact us promptly so we can correct file problems, redeliver exports, or discuss a reasonable adjustment.

5. Revisions instead of refunds

When a delivered file has a technical issue or does not match the accepted scope, ReadyMadeVideo may provide a correction, replacement export, or reasonable revision. New creative direction, new listing facts, staging changes, access limitations, or requests outside the accepted scope may require additional fees.

6. How to request help

For refund, rescheduling, or delivery questions, contact contact@readymadevideo.com with the property address, package, shoot date, and a short description of the issue.
