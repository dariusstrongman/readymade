Shot plan

How the shoot is built.

Every Drone Only flight has a clear purpose before takeoff: front approach, rear reveal, top-down context, and neighborhood positioning when relevant.

1Front approach

A low, smooth approach establishes the property from the street or entry direction.

2Rear reveal

A controlled movement shows backyard, pool, patio, lot depth, and exterior living features.

3Top-down context

A straight-down or high oblique angle helps show lot shape, roofline, setbacks, and nearby boundaries.

4Neighborhood position

When appropriate, the package captures nearby parks, water, golf, schools, or community amenities without overpromising proximity.

A practical aerial route.Drone coverage is planned around safe, legal, useful property context: arrival, lot shape, rear condition, amenities, and surrounding location cues.

What do you need from me?

Property address, access instructions, preferred shoot window, listing notes, add-ons, and any seller or HOA constraints that affect the shoot.

When do I receive it?

Standard packages target a next-morning handoff after the shoot when access, scope, weather, and technical conditions allow. Larger scopes and weather-sensitive aerial work are confirmed before scheduling.

Can the package change?

Yes. If the listing needs more coverage, drone, twilight, staging, or extra social cuts, the scope can be adjusted before the appointment is approved.
